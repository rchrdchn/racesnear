# Privacy

{% updated %}

<p class="lead">Short version: no accounts, no ad trackers, and we never sell data.</p>

- **Analytics.** We may use Cloudflare Web Analytics, which counts page views without cookies or personal profiles.
- **Newsletter.** If you subscribe, your email address and chosen city are stored by our email provider (beehiiv) so we can send you that edition. Every email has a one-click unsubscribe.
- **Registration links.** When you click Register you leave this site for the race's registration platform, which has its own privacy policy. Some links carry a referral code so the platform knows we sent you.
- **Email.** If you email us, we keep the conversation to reply and for our records.
